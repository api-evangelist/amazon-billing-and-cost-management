We’re excited to announce Managed Dashboards in AWS Billing and Cost Management (BCM). Starting today, a collection of preconfigured, read-only dashboards appear automatically in your BCM Dashboards list. They are populated with your account data and ready to deliver actionable cost insights the moment you open them.
