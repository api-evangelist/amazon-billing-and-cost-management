AWS Data Exports lets you select which columns to include in your AWS Cost and Usage Reports (CUR 2.0) export. But filtering down rows to specific accounts or services still required post-processing. Whether you need to share cost data with a partner, isolate a program's spend, or meet an audit scope, teams often build downstream pipelines with AWS Lambda, AWS Glue, or Amazon Athena.
