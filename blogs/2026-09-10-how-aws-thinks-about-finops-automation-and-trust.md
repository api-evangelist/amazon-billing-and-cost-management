AWS introduced the FinOps Agent (currently in public preview) at FinOps X 2026. FinOps automation is still relatively new, and given the sensitivity around cloud and AI cost management, it's understandable if you're hesitant to go all-in on autonomous FinOps right away. In this post, we'll share our mental model for FinOps automation, the four tiers of automation you can follow, and the four built-in trust levers you can rely on to stay in control as automation scales.
