Implementing a FinOps weekly checkpoint is a proactive approach to catch spend trends sooner and generates more meaningful insights for your monthly variance analysis. Gathering variance context throughout the month leads to a clearer, more impactful analysis. This weekly checkpoint involves regularly reviewing cost data, identifying anomalies or trends, and engaging with technical teams to understand cost change drivers.
