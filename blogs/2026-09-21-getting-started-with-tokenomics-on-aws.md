Tokens are the currency models work in. Every model takes tokens as input and produces tokens as output, and those tokens can come from anywhere: a person typing a prompt, an application making a call, or another AI agent talking to your model through an MCP server. In this blog, we focus on the tokens generated by AI coding tools and AI solutions.
