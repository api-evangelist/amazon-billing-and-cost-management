When you use AWS Billing Conductor (ABC) to produce pro forma billing, you have full control over how costs are presented to your member accounts. By default, commitments purchased at the management account level are managed separately from ABC billing groups. Pro forma bills for your member accounts reflect on-demand equivalent costs.
